Privacy Policy

AI Music Generator · last updated: 24 November 2026

Who uses this application

AI Music Generator is a personal desktop tool. It is used only by its owner to publish music videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and other people cannot sign in to it.

What data the application accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the application uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel and add them to the owner's playlists,
  • read basic information about the owner's channel and videos (titles, view counts),
  • read YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's channel (views, watch time, traffic sources).

The application does not access data of any other YouTube or Google user.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token and all reports are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party, and no data is sold or shared. The application contains no advertising or tracking.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also stops all access.

YouTube and Google policies

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
