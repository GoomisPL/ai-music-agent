AI Music Generator

Personal desktop tool for its owner's YouTube channel.

AI Music Generator (also called AI Music Agent) runs on its owner's Windows computer. It creates instrumental music with local AI models, renders a cover picture and a video, and uploads the result to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. All AI models run locally; no cloud AI service is used.

The application is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer sign-in to other people and does not collect data from other users.
